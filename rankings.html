Fiscal Year Size Category What do size categories mean?
Peer size categories group agencies by annual request volume so scores are compared against similar-scale peers, not government-wide. Categories vary slightly by year as reporting bands were refined, but generally follow: Micro / Very Small (roughly 1–99 requests/year), Small (100–1,000), Medium (1,000–10,000), and Large (10,000+). "No Activity" agencies reported no processed requests that year; "No Data" agencies didn't report enough to classify.
